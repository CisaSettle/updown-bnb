01What we are building我们在做什么

A non-custodial, on-chain binary option (Up/Down) market on BNB Smart Chain, settled by Chainlink price feeds and priced by a parimutuel two-sided pool rather than by a house or an order book.

A user picks UP or DOWN on the price of an asset over a fixed round. When the round locks, the Chainlink price is recorded as the strike (lockPrice); when it closes, the Chainlink price is recorded as the settlement (closePrice). The winning side splits the losing side's pool pro-rata, minus a protocol fee. Nobody can lose more than their stake, and nobody — including the protocol owner — can touch a user's principal.

Markets in v1
BTC, ETH and BNB against USD, each over a 1-minute and a 10-minute round — six markets, every one settled in USDT. One contract instance = one (asset, duration) pair.
Price source
Chainlink AggregatorV3 on BSC, 8 decimals. Read on-chain, verifiable by anyone.
Pricing
Parimutuel. Odds are the pool ratio, which is the implied probability.
Worst case for a user
Lose the stake. There is no leverage, no liquidation and no margin call.

这是一个部署在 BNB 智能链上的非托管、全链上二元期权（涨/跌）市场： 由 Chainlink 预言机喂价完成结算，由双边平价池（parimutuel）决定赔率，而不是由庄家 报价或订单簿撮合。

用户在一个固定长度的轮次内选择看涨（UP）或看跌（DOWN）。轮次锁定时， 合约记录当时的 Chainlink 价格作为行权价（lockPrice）；轮次结束时，记录 当时的价格作为结算价（closePrice）。获胜一方按下注比例瓜分失败方的资金池， 并扣除协议手续费。用户的最大亏损就是本金，而且任何人——包括协议管理员——都无法动用用户本金。

03Why parimutuel, and not the other two models为什么选择平价池，而不是另外两种模式

  1. A fixed payout ratio (the Binance model) needs house capital and a volatility model. Someone has to take the other side of every trade and price it correctly. On-chain, that role either burns capital or gets arbitraged by anyone with a faster price feed. It is not viable for a cold start with no treasury.
  2. A CLOB (the Polymarket model) needs off-chain matching and professional market makers. Polymarket has liquidity because it has dedicated MMs quoting its books. An empty order book is an empty product, and we cannot bootstrap that relationship on day one.
  3. A parimutuel pool needs exactly zero starting liquidity. The first two traders make a market for each other. The resulting odds are exactly the implied probability — the same information a Polymarket share price carries — the whole mechanism fits on-chain, there is no counterparty to default, and a bet on BSC costs well under one cent of gas.
The synthesis

Polymarket's on-chain, oracle-settled, implied-probability semantics · Binance's multi-duration, one-tap binary UX · and a pool mechanism that actually works on BSC from day one with no treasury and no market maker.

The cost of this choice is honest and worth stating: the payout ratio is not known when you bet, because it depends on what everyone else does before lock, and there is no early exit. In exchange, the market can exist at all without a house.

  1. 固定赔付倍率（币安模式）需要庄家资本和一套波动率模型。 必须有人为每一笔交易充当对手方并给出正确报价。放到链上，这个角色要么持续烧钱，要么被任何拥有更快 价格源的人套利。对一个没有金库的冷启动项目来说不可行。
  2. 中央限价订单簿（Polymarket 模式）需要链下撮合和专业做市商。 Polymarket 之所以有流动性，是因为有专职做市商持续挂单。空的订单簿等同于没有产品，而我们无法在第一天 就建立起这种合作关系。
  3. 平价池所需的初始流动性恰好为零。最先进场的两个交易者就互为对手方。由此得到的赔率 正好等于隐含概率——与 Polymarket 份额价格所承载的信息完全一致；整套机制可以完整放在链上， 不存在会违约的交易对手；而在 BSC 上下注一次的 Gas 成本远低于一美分。
综合结论

取 Polymarket 的链上化、预言机结算与隐含概率语义 · 取币安的多周期、一键式二元交易体验 · 再加上一套无需金库、无需做市商、在 BSC 上第一天就能跑通的资金池机制。

这一选择的代价也应如实说明：下注时并不知道最终赔付倍率，因为它取决于锁定前所有人的下注行为；同时不支持 提前离场。换来的是——这个市场根本不需要庄家就能存在。

04Round lifecycle轮次生命周期

One market instance = one (asset, duration) pair. interval is the round duration in seconds. Every round has a betting phase of one interval followed by a holding phase of one interval, so at any wall-clock moment exactly one epoch is accepting bets and exactly one epoch is live.

一个市场实例对应一组（资产，周期）。interval 是以秒计的轮次周期。每一轮先经历一个 interval 的下注阶段，再经历一个 interval 的持仓阶段。因此 在任意时刻，恰好有一轮在接受下注，也恰好有一轮正在进行中。

The timestamp grid

Round boundaries are derived from an immutable anchor rather than from the previous round's actual settlement time, so they can never drift, no matter how late the crank is turned: 

startTs(N) = anchorTs + (N - epochAnchor) * interval
lockTs(N)  = startTs(N) + interval
closeTs(N) = lockTs(N)  + interval

therefore  lockTs(N) == closeTs(N-1)

When funded risk needs a boundary transaction, executeRound(boundaryRoundId) does three things atomically from the one shared-boundary price. Empty grid slots stay virtually open without a keeper transaction; the first bet materialises the current epoch:

  1. close epoch N−1 — closePrice(N−1) = price, compute the reward pool
  2. lock epoch N — lockPrice(N) = price, betting is now shut
  3. open epoch N+1 for betting

Deterministic settlement — nobody holds a timing option

The price of a boundary is the last Chainlink print at or before that boundary timestamp — not whatever latestRoundData() happens to return when someone gets round to calling. The caller passes the round id of that print and the contract proves it is the last qualifying one before accepting it.

Once a boundary has passed, the set of prints before it is frozen, so the settlement price is a pure function of the boundary timestamp. Calling one second after the boundary and calling three minutes after it produce byte-identical outcomes. That property is what makes the next one safe.

Liveness: executeRound is permissionless

  • Anyone may call it, from lockTs onwards. There is no operator role, no privileged settler, and no address — including the project's own keeper — that can influence a settlement price.
  • Lateness cannot buy anyone a better outcome; the only thing it can do is run out the round's snapshotted bufferSeconds, after which the round voids into full refunds instead of settling.
  • The people with money on the winning side have the incentive to call, because settling is what makes their claim payable. That is what keeps the market live without a trusted role.
  • If nobody calls at all, a stuck round becomes refundable on its own timer — refundable() flips to true with no admin action required.
  • A single call also fast-forwards past a long outage: the epoch counter jumps straight to the epoch the wall clock is actually in, in one transaction.
What an outage costs

Users get their money back, never less. Nobody calling degrades the product into refunds; it can never produce a wrong settlement or a loss. The project runs a keeper because someone should turn the crank promptly — punctuality is a product-quality requirement, not a safety one.

时间网格

轮次边界由一个不可变的锚点推导得出，而不是由上一轮的实际结算时刻推导，因此无论结算调用迟到多久， 边界都不会漂移： 

startTs(N) = anchorTs + (N - epochAnchor) * interval
lockTs(N)  = startTs(N) + interval
closeTs(N) = lockTs(N)  + interval

由此可得  lockTs(N) == closeTs(N-1)

只有真实资金需要边界交易时，executeRound(boundaryRoundId) 才使用共享边界的 同一个价格原子完成三件事。空的时间网格轮次无需 keeper 交易也会保持虚拟开盘， 第一笔下注才把当前轮次写上链：

  1. 结束轮次 N−1——写入 closePrice(N−1) = price，计算奖池
  2. 锁定轮次 N——写入 lockPrice(N) = price，下注就此关闭
  3. 开放轮次 N+1 的下注

确定性结算——没有人握有时机期权

某个边界的价格，被定义为该边界时间戳之前（含）的最后一次 Chainlink 报价——而不是某人 终于想起来调用时 latestRoundData() 恰好返回的那个值。调用者需要把这条报价的轮次 ID 传进来， 合约会先证明它确实是符合条件的最后一条，然后才接受。

边界一旦过去，它之前的报价集合就被冻结了，因此结算价是边界时间戳的纯函数。在边界后一秒调用与在边界后 三分钟调用，得到的结果逐字节相同。正是这一性质，让下面这件事变得安全。

活性：executeRound 无需许可

  • 任何人都可以从 lockTs 起调用它。不存在 operator 角色，不存在特权结算方， 也没有任何地址——包括项目方自己的 keeper——能够影响结算价。
  • 迟到并不能为任何人换来更好的结果；它唯一能造成的后果，是耗尽该轮快照中的 bufferSeconds，此后该轮不再结算，而是作废并全额退款。
  • 钱押在获胜一方的人天然有动力去调用，因为只有完成结算，他们的奖金才可领取。这就是这个市场在没有 可信角色的情况下依然保持活跃的原因。
  • 即便完全无人调用，卡住的轮次也会按自身的计时器自动变为可退款——refundable() 会自行 转为 true，不需要任何管理员操作。
  • 一次调用还能一次性追平长时间的停机：轮次计数会在同一笔交易里直接跳到当前墙钟所处的轮次。
停机的代价

用户拿回全部本金，一分不少。无人调用只会把产品降级为「退款」，绝不会产生错误结算或资金损失。 项目方仍然运行一个 keeper，是因为应该有人及时转动这个曲柄——准时性是产品体验要求， 而不是安全要求。

05Settlement & payout maths结算与赔付计算

Let up be the total staked UP, down the total staked DOWN, and feeBps the protocol fee in basis points (default 300 = 3%, hard-capped at 1000 = 10%).

Outcome

closePrice > lockPrice   →  UP wins
closePrice < lockPrice   →  DOWN wins
closePrice == lockPrice  →  tie  →  round is voided, full refund, zero fee

When a round is voided (full refund, zero fee)

  • a tie — closePrice == lockPrice
  • a one-sided book — up == 0 || down == 0: there is no counterparty, so there is nothing to win and nothing is taken
  • the round's settlement window — its own boundary plus its snapshotted bufferSeconds — elapsed without settlement. This is how a dead feed reaches a refund: no usable print means no valid proof, and a call with no valid proof reverts rather than voiding, so the round only voids later, on the timer
  • the admin called pause() before the round locked — it never received a strike, so nobody could have known its outcome, and it runs out its window and refunds in full

A round that had already locked when a pause landed is deliberately not on that list. executeRound is not whenNotPaused: the locked round settles straight through the pause, at the price the feed actually printed, and the winner can claim while the market is paused. See section 7.

A voided round pays every participant exactly their stake back. Void and settled are mutually exclusive for payout purposes: once a round resolves it is exactly one of the two — never both payable and refundable, and never a round whose stakes are stuck as neither.

Otherwise — the parimutuel split

fee        = losePool * feeBps / 10000     ← charged ONLY on the losing pool
rewardPool = winPool + losePool - fee
rewardBase = winPool
payout     = userStake * rewardPool / rewardBase
           = userStake + userStake/winPool * losePool * (1 - feeBps/10000)

Why the fee is charged only on the losing pool

The common on-chain implementation charges the fee on the total pool, which silently taxes the winner's own principal. On a 1,000 / 1,000 book with a 3% fee, a 100 stake on the winning side returns 194 under fee-on-total, but 197 under our fee-on-loser. The winner is never paid less than their principal, and the odds the UI displayed are the odds actually paid. This is a deliberate, user-favourable deviation.

Displayed odds

The same information, expressed in both reference products' vocabularies. odds(epoch) returns basis points of a 1× multiple, where 10000 = 1.0000×, and returns (0, 0) while either side is still empty. 

upMultiple    = (up + down * (1 - fee)) / up      Binance-style "payout ratio"
upImpliedProb = 1 / upMultiple                    Polymarket-style "share price", 0..1

Worked example

Note that Alice's own stake is part of the winning pool, so adding to a crowded side dilutes its own odds. This is the parimutuel property, and it is why the UI shows live odds that move as the book fills.

设 up 为看涨方总下注额，down 为看跌方总下注额，feeBps 为以基点 计的协议手续费（默认 300，即 3%；硬上限 1000，即 10%）。

胜负判定

closePrice > lockPrice   →  看涨方获胜
closePrice < lockPrice   →  看跌方获胜
closePrice == lockPrice  →  平局  →  该轮作废，全额退款，零手续费

哪些情况会导致轮次作废（全额退款、零手续费）

  • 平局——closePrice == lockPrice
  • 单边盘——up == 0 || down == 0：没有交易对手，因此无从赢起，也不会拿走任何人的钱
  • 该轮的结算窗口——自身边界加上快照的 bufferSeconds——已过而仍未结算。喂价中断正是通过这条路径走向退款：没有可用报价就构造不出有效证明，而没有有效证明的调用是直接回滚而不是作废，所以该轮只会在时限到达后才作废
  • 管理员在该轮锁定之前调用了 pause()——它从未拿到行权价，谁都不可能预知它的结果，于是耗尽时限后全额退款

暂停落下时已经锁定的轮次刻意不在这张清单里。executeRound 没有 whenNotPaused 修饰：已锁定的轮次会径直穿过暂停，按喂价真实报出的价格结算，赢家在市场 暂停期间照样可以领取。详见第 7 节。

作废轮次会把每个参与者的本金原数退回。就赔付而言，「作废」与「已结算」互斥：一轮一旦有了结果，就必然 落在两者之一——既不会既可领奖又可退款，也不会两者皆不可、把本金卡死在合约里。

其余情况——平价池分配

fee        = losePool * feeBps / 10000     ← 手续费只从输方资金池扣取
rewardPool = winPool + losePool - fee
rewardBase = winPool
payout     = userStake * rewardPool / rewardBase
           = userStake + userStake/winPool * losePool * (1 - feeBps/10000)

为什么手续费只从输方资金池扣取

链上常见的实现是按总池收取手续费，这实际上悄悄地对赢家自己的本金征了税。以 1,000 / 1,000 的 盘口、3% 手续费为例，赢方的 100 本金在「按总池收费」下只拿回 194，而在我们的 「只对输方池收费」下拿回 197。赢家拿到的钱永远不会少于本金，界面显示的赔率也正是 实际兑付的赔率。这是一处有意为之、对用户更有利的偏离。

06Oracle rules预言机规则

Both the strike and the settlement are Chainlink AggregatorV3 prints read on-chain at 8 decimals. A boundary timestamp T resolves to the answer of the Chainlink round whose updatedAt is the last one at or before T. The caller supplies that round id and the contract proves it.

What the contract checks before it accepts a price

  • getRoundData(roundId) returns that exact id, a positive answer, and a non-zero updatedAt that is not in the future — the call is wrapped in try/catch, so a reverting feed simply fails the check
  • updatedAt <= T — the print is at or before the boundary, never after it
  • T - updatedAt <= oracleMaxAge — the feed was genuinely alive at the boundary rather than merely last seen at some point. oracleMaxAge is immutable, so this budget cannot be widened after the fact
  • the id belongs to the market's pinned oraclePhase. Chainlink proxies encode roundId = phaseId << 64 | aggregatorRoundId, and a print from any other phase is not evidence about this market's price at all
  • it is the last qualifying print within that phase: either getRoundData(roundId + 1) does not exist, or its updatedAt is already past T
  • the transaction itself arrives strictly after the boundary second, and no later than the boundary plus the round's snapshotted bufferSeconds

If any check fails the call reverts with InvalidBoundaryProof and nothing changes — it does not void the round. That is what stops a losing bettor from front-running an honest call with a bogus round id to force everyone into a refund. A round that genuinely cannot be priced reaches its refund the slow way, when its own settlement window runs out. The contract never falls back to a stale price, an interpolated price, or an admin-supplied price.

The price source cannot be changed, and neither can the phase

oracle is immutable and there is no setOracle. A settable price source is a path from the admin key straight to the settlement price of a round that is already locked: pause, point the market at a feed you control, settle at a price of your choosing, point it back, unpause — one atomic transaction from a multisig, taking the whole opposing pool. A locked position has no exit, so no timelock mitigates it. On mainnet the address is a Chainlink proxy, whose address is stable by design, so nothing is given up.

The market is likewise bound to one aggregator phase for life (oraclePhase, immutable). A proxy can confirm a replacement aggregator that already carries history timestamped before the switch, after which two different ids both look like "the last print at or before the boundary" and the caller would pick. Pinning removes the choice. If the feed genuinely moves on, nothing can be proved: every round times out into a full refund, the market retires, and a new one is deployed against the new feed.

Why a dead feed cannot manufacture a fake tie

oracleMaxAge < interval is enforced in the constructor. Two consecutive boundaries are one interval apart, so they can never both resolve to the same Chainlink print. A frozen feed therefore voids the round into refunds instead of producing closePrice == lockPrice and calling it a tie.

合约在接受一个价格之前会检查什么

  • getRoundData(roundId) 返回的 ID 与传入值完全一致、报价为正数、updatedAt 非零且不在未来——调用被 try/catch 包裹，因此会回滚的喂价只会导致检查失败
  • updatedAt <= T——报价发生在边界之前（含），绝不能在边界之后
  • T - updatedAt <= oracleMaxAge——喂价在边界时刻确实是活的，而不只是「某个时点曾经更新过」。oracleMaxAge 是不可变的，因此这个时限无法事后放宽
  • 该 ID 必须属于本市场绑定的 oraclePhase。Chainlink 代理把轮次 ID 编码为 roundId = phaseId << 64 | aggregatorRoundId，来自其他相位的报价，根本不能作为本市场价格的证据
  • 它必须是该相位内符合条件的最后一条：要么 getRoundData(roundId + 1) 不存在，要么其 updatedAt 已经晚于 T
  • 交易本身必须严格晚于边界那一秒，且不得晚于「边界 + 该轮快照的 bufferSeconds」

任一检查未通过，调用会以 InvalidBoundaryProof 直接回滚，什么都不改变——它 并不会作废该轮。正是这一点，挡住了眼看要输的下注者用一个伪造 ID 抢先调用、把整轮打成全额退款。真正 无法定价的轮次只能走慢路：等它自己的结算时限耗尽。合约绝不会退而使用过期价格、插值价格，或由管理员 手工提供的价格。

价格来源不能更改，绑定的相位也不能

oracle 是 immutable，合约里没有 setOracle。一个可设置的 价格来源，等于给管理员私钥开了一条直通已锁定轮次结算价的路：暂停、把市场指向一个自己 控制的喂价、按任意价格结算、再指回去、解除暂停——多签一笔原子交易就能拿走整个对手池。已锁定的 仓位没有退出通道，因此时间锁也救不了。主网上这个地址指向的是 Chainlink 代理合约，其 地址本就设计为稳定不变，所以什么也没有牺牲。

市场同样被终身绑定在一个聚合器相位上（oraclePhase，不可变）。代理可以确认一个新的 聚合器，而它已经携带了切换之前时间戳的历史；此后会有两个不同的 ID 都像是「边界之前的最后 一条报价」，由调用者挑选。绑定相位就消灭了这个选择权。如果喂价真的换代了，任何价格都无法被证明： 每一轮都会耗尽时限、全额退款，市场就此退役，并针对新喂价重新部署一个新的市场。

为什么「死掉的喂价」无法伪造出平局

构造函数强制 oracleMaxAge < interval。相邻两个边界相隔正好一个 interval， 因此它们不可能解析到同一条 Chainlink 报价。喂价冻结时，该轮会作废退款，而不会得出 closePrice == lockPrice 并被当成平局。

The non-custodial invariant

The contract tracks outstanding, an upper bound on the user funds it still owes, and this must hold at every block. It is enforced by a Foundry invariant suite, not just asserted here: 

assetBalance(market) >= outstanding + treasuryAmount

outstanding = Σ unsettled stakes + Σ unclaimed payouts

It is deliberately an upper bound rather than an exact figure: per-winner floor division can leave at most one settlement unit (1 wei) per winner in the contract. That residue is never paid out and is not withdrawable by anyone, including the admin, which keeps the solvency check conservative in the users' favour.

Funds only ever leave by pull payment — the user calls claim(epochs[]), or claimTo(epochs[], to) if they are a contract account that cannot receive the settlement asset itself. The contract never pushes money to a user during settlement, so one hostile or broken recipient can never block settlement for everyone else.

claimFor(user, epochs[]) lets anyone pay the gas to collect on someone's behalf, with the contract paying that user, at their own address — the caller cannot redirect a single wei. It requires the holder to have opted in with setAutoClaimOptIn(true), and the opt-in applies to every account with no inference about account type. That is not caution for its own sake: an address reports no code both while its constructor is still running and after it has self-destructed, so no opcode reliably tells a wallet apart from a contract that cannot spend from its own address. Pushing to one of those would strand its winnings and cancel the claimTo it had planned, so the contract asks rather than guesses.

assetBalance(market) >= outstanding + treasuryAmount

这里刻意取「上界」而非精确值：逐个赢家的向下取整最多会在合约里各留下一个结算单位（1 wei）的零头。 这部分零头永远不会被支付，也不会被任何人（包括管理员）提走，从而使偿付能力校验始终保守地偏向用户一侧。

资金只通过拉取式提款离开合约——由用户自己调用 claim(epochs[])； 若下注者是无法接收结算资产的合约账户，则调用 claimTo(epochs[], to) 指定收款地址。 合约在结算过程中从不主动向用户打款，因此单个恶意或异常的收款地址永远无法阻塞其他所有人的结算。

claimFor(user, epochs[]) 允许任何人自掏 gas 替他人领取，而合约把钱付给该用户本人、 打到他自己的地址——调用者一个 wei 都改不了流向。前提是持有人已通过 setAutoClaimOptIn(true) 自行开启，且这一开关对所有账户一视同仁，不对账户类型做任何推断。 这并非多余的谨慎：一个地址在自己的构造函数尚未跑完时、以及自毁之后，都会报告"没有代码"， 因此没有任何操作码能可靠区分普通钱包与"无法从自身地址花钱的合约"。 把钱推给后者会让它的赔付彻底卡死，还会取消它本已安排好的 claimTo， 所以合约选择先问，而不是猜。

第 6 轮 —— Codex（gpt-5.6-sol），2026-08-26 → 要求整改，代码问题已修复

等级问题修复方式
严重 暂停加上 setOracle，等于完全掌控结算价。把结算与暂停解耦之后，已锁定的轮次会按市场 「当下读到的那个喂价」结算——而 setOracle 是 onlyOwner whenPaused。于是 pause → setOracle（恶意喂价）→ executeRound（伪造 ID）→ setOracle（改回）→ unpause，就能把一个已锁定轮次的结算价写成任意值，多签一笔 原子交易完成，拿走整个对手池。原来的相位校验只比较轮次 ID 的高 16 位，而攻击者自己的喂价想返回什么就 返回什么。一次独立评审端到端复现了它：5 万本金获利 24.4 万 USDT，原生 BNB 市场同样中招。第 5 轮的 修复，等于用一份无上限的盗取，换掉了一份有界的取消期权。 这件事没有任何角色或延时能够约束：已锁定的仓位没有退出通道，交易落地那一刻锁着什么就被拿走什么。 oracle 现在是 immutable，setOracle 已删除。实际上什么也没有 牺牲——主网上这个地址指向 Chainlink 代理合约，其地址本就设计为稳定不变；而一个真正死掉的喂价， 现在会让每一轮通过作废路径全额退款。
中 只比较两个已存 ID 的相位，并不能消除排序依赖。在代理确认之前执行，上一轮按旧价格 A 结算、当前轮 也按 A 锁定；先确认再执行，上一轮因相位不一致而退款，而没有相位校验的 _lockRound 会把当前轮 锁在追溯出来的价格 B 上。 市场现在被终身绑定在一个聚合器相位上（oraclePhase，不可变）。来自其他相位的报价在任何位置 都不是有效证明，会直接回滚而不是作废，因此眼看要输的下注者也无法用一个跨相位 ID 取消轮次。最后一条 报价的判定改为相位内判定：后继就是下一个 ID，若它不存在，则候选就是该相位的最后一条。回归测试： test_roundSpanningAnAggregatorPhaseChangeRefunds。
