01What we are building我们在做什么

A non-custodial, on-chain binary option (Up/Down) market on BNB Smart Chain, settled by Chainlink price feeds and priced by a parimutuel two-sided pool rather than by a house or an order book.

A user picks UP or DOWN on the price of an asset over a fixed round. When the round locks, the Chainlink price is recorded as the strike (lockPrice); when it closes, the Chainlink price is recorded as the settlement (closePrice). The winning side splits the losing side's pool pro-rata, minus a protocol fee. Nobody can lose more than their stake, and nobody — including the protocol owner — can touch a user's principal.

Markets in v1
BTC/USD 5m and BTC/USD 1h settled in USDT, plus BNB/USD 5m settled in native BNB. One contract instance = one (asset, duration) pair.
Price source
Chainlink AggregatorV3 on BSC, 8 decimals. Read on-chain, verifiable by anyone.
Pricing
Parimutuel. Odds are the pool ratio, which is the implied probability.
Worst case for a user
Lose the stake. There is no leverage, no liquidation and no margin call.

这是一个部署在 BNB 智能链上的非托管、全链上二元期权（涨/跌）市场： 由 Chainlink 预言机喂价完成结算，由双边平价池（parimutuel）决定赔率，而不是由庄家 报价或订单簿撮合。

用户在一个固定长度的轮次内选择看涨（UP）或看跌（DOWN）。轮次锁定时， 合约记录当时的 Chainlink 价格作为行权价（lockPrice）；轮次结束时，记录 当时的价格作为结算价（closePrice）。获胜一方按下注比例瓜分失败方的资金池， 并扣除协议手续费。用户的最大亏损就是本金，而且任何人——包括协议管理员——都无法动用用户本金。

价格来源
BSC 上的 Chainlink AggregatorV3 喂价，8 位小数。链上读取，人人可核验。
定价方式
平价池。赔率即资金池比例，也就是市场的隐含概率。
用户最坏结果
损失本金。没有杠杆、没有强平、没有追加保证金。

03Why parimutuel, and not the other two models为什么选择平价池，而不是另外两种模式

  1. A fixed payout ratio (the Binance model) needs house capital and a volatility model. Someone has to take the other side of every trade and price it correctly. On-chain, that role either burns capital or gets arbitraged by anyone with a faster price feed. It is not viable for a cold start with no treasury.
  2. A CLOB (the Polymarket model) needs off-chain matching and professional market makers. Polymarket has liquidity because it has dedicated MMs quoting its books. An empty order book is an empty product, and we cannot bootstrap that relationship on day one.
  3. A parimutuel pool needs exactly zero starting liquidity. The first two traders make a market for each other. The resulting odds are exactly the implied probability — the same information a Polymarket share price carries — the whole mechanism fits on-chain, there is no counterparty to default, and a bet on BSC costs well under one cent of gas.
The synthesis

Polymarket's on-chain, oracle-settled, implied-probability semantics · Binance's multi-duration, one-tap binary UX · and a pool mechanism that actually works on BSC from day one with no treasury and no market maker.

The cost of this choice is honest and worth stating: the payout ratio is not known when you bet, because it depends on what everyone else does before lock, and there is no early exit. In exchange, the market can exist at all without a house.

  1. 固定赔付倍率（币安模式）需要庄家资本和一套波动率模型。 必须有人为每一笔交易充当对手方并给出正确报价。放到链上，这个角色要么持续烧钱，要么被任何拥有更快 价格源的人套利。对一个没有金库的冷启动项目来说不可行。
  2. 中央限价订单簿（Polymarket 模式）需要链下撮合和专业做市商。 Polymarket 之所以有流动性，是因为有专职做市商持续挂单。空的订单簿等同于没有产品，而我们无法在第一天 就建立起这种合作关系。
  3. 平价池所需的初始流动性恰好为零。最先进场的两个交易者就互为对手方。由此得到的赔率 正好等于隐含概率——与 Polymarket 份额价格所承载的信息完全一致；整套机制可以完整放在链上， 不存在会违约的交易对手；而在 BSC 上下注一次的 Gas 成本远低于一美分。
综合结论

取 Polymarket 的链上化、预言机结算与隐含概率语义 · 取币安的多周期、一键式二元交易体验 · 再加上一套无需金库、无需做市商、在 BSC 上第一天就能跑通的资金池机制。

这一选择的代价也应如实说明：下注时并不知道最终赔付倍率，因为它取决于锁定前所有人的下注行为；同时不支持 提前离场。换来的是——这个市场根本不需要庄家就能存在。

04Round lifecycle轮次生命周期

One market instance = one (asset, duration) pair. interval is the round duration in seconds. Every round has a betting phase of one interval followed by a holding phase of one interval, so at any wall-clock moment exactly one epoch is accepting bets and exactly one epoch is live.

一个市场实例对应一组（资产，周期）。interval 是以秒计的轮次周期。每一轮先经历一个 interval 的下注阶段，再经历一个 interval 的持仓阶段。因此 在任意时刻，恰好有一轮在接受下注，也恰好有一轮正在进行中。

The timestamp grid

Round boundaries are derived from an immutable anchor rather than from the previous round's actual settlement time, so they can never drift, no matter how late the keeper is: 

startTs(N) = anchorTs + (N - epochAnchor) * interval
lockTs(N)  = startTs(N) + interval
closeTs(N) = lockTs(N)  + interval

therefore  lockTs(N) == closeTs(N-1)

executeRound() is called once per interval and does three things atomically, with a single oracle read, so two adjacent rounds always agree on the boundary price:

  1. close epoch N−1 — closePrice(N−1) = price, compute the reward pool
  2. lock epoch N — lockPrice(N) = price, betting is now shut
  3. open epoch N+1 for betting

Liveness: the keeper is not a single point of failure

  • The operator (the keeper bot) may call executeRound() from lockTs onwards.
  • Once lockTs + bufferSeconds has passed, anyone may call it. But a call that late can no longer settle at a price from the right moment, so it can only void the affected rounds into full refunds. A stranger can never choose a settlement price.
  • If nobody calls at all, a stuck round becomes refundable on its own timer — refundable() flips to true with no admin action required.
  • A single call also fast-forwards past a long keeper outage: the epoch counter jumps straight to the epoch the wall clock is actually in, in one transaction.
What a keeper outage costs

Users get their money back, never less. A keeper outage degrades the product into refunds; it can never produce a wrong settlement or a loss. Keeper punctuality is a product-quality requirement, not a safety one.

时间栅格

轮次边界由一个不可变的锚点推导得出，而不是由上一轮的实际结算时刻推导，因此无论 keeper 迟到多久， 边界都不会漂移： 

startTs(N) = anchorTs + (N - epochAnchor) * interval
lockTs(N)  = startTs(N) + interval
closeTs(N) = lockTs(N)  + interval

由此可得  lockTs(N) == closeTs(N-1)

executeRound() 每个 interval 调用一次，用一次预言机读取 原子地完成三件事，从而保证相邻两轮在边界价格上永远一致：

  1. 结束轮次 N−1——写入 closePrice(N−1) = price，计算奖池
  2. 锁定轮次 N——写入 lockPrice(N) = price，下注就此关闭
  3. 开放轮次 N+1 的下注

活性：keeper 不是单点故障

  • operator（keeper 机器人）可以从 lockTs 起调用 executeRound()。
  • 一旦超过 lockTs + bufferSeconds，任何人都可以调用。但这么晚的调用 已经无法拿到属于正确时刻的价格，因此它只能把相关轮次作废并全额退款。陌生人永远 无法挑选结算价。
  • 即便无人调用，卡住的轮次也会按自身的计时器自动变为可退款——refundable() 会自行 转为 true，不需要任何管理员操作。
  • 一次调用还能一次性追平长时间的 keeper 停机：轮次计数会在同一笔交易里直接跳到当前墙钟所处的轮次。
keeper 停机的代价

用户拿回全部本金，一分不少。keeper 停机只会把产品降级为「退款」，绝不会产生错误结算或资金损失。 keeper 的准时性是产品体验要求，而不是安全要求。

05Settlement & payout maths结算与赔付计算

Let up be the total staked UP, down the total staked DOWN, and feeBps the protocol fee in basis points (default 300 = 3%, hard-capped at 1000 = 10%).

Outcome

closePrice > lockPrice   →  UP wins
closePrice < lockPrice   →  DOWN wins
closePrice == lockPrice  →  tie  →  round is voided, full refund, zero fee

When a round is voided (full refund, zero fee)

  • a tie — closePrice == lockPrice
  • a one-sided book — up == 0 || down == 0: there is no counterparty, so there is nothing to win and nothing is taken
  • the oracle was unusable at lock or at close (stale, non-positive, or a replayed round id)
  • the settlement window lockTs + bufferSeconds elapsed without settlement
  • the admin called pause() while the round was live

A voided round pays every participant exactly their stake back. Void and settled are mutually exclusive for payout purposes: no round is ever both payable and refundable, and no round is ever neither.

Otherwise — the parimutuel split

fee        = losePool * feeBps / 10000     ← charged ONLY on the losing pool
rewardPool = winPool + losePool - fee
rewardBase = winPool
payout     = userStake * rewardPool / rewardBase
           = userStake + userStake/winPool * losePool * (1 - feeBps/10000)

Why the fee is charged only on the losing pool

The common on-chain implementation charges the fee on the total pool, which silently taxes the winner's own principal. On a 1,000 / 1,000 book with a 3% fee, a 100 stake on the winning side returns 194 under fee-on-total, but 197 under our fee-on-loser. The winner is never paid less than their principal, and the odds the UI displayed are the odds actually paid. This is a deliberate, user-favourable deviation.

Displayed odds

The same information, expressed in both reference products' vocabularies. odds(epoch) returns basis points of a 1× multiple, where 10000 = 1.0000×, and returns (0, 0) while either side is still empty. 

upMultiple    = (up + down * (1 - fee)) / up      Binance-style "payout ratio"
upImpliedProb = 1 / upMultiple                    Polymarket-style "share price", 0..1

Worked example

A 5-minute BTC round with a 3% fee. Alice stakes 100 USDT on UP; the book closes at 1,100 UP against 3,000 DOWN. BTC ends higher, so UP wins.

Note that Alice's own stake is part of the winning pool, so adding to a crowded side dilutes its own odds. This is the parimutuel property, and it is why the UI shows live odds that move as the book fills.

设 up 为看涨方总下注额，down 为看跌方总下注额，feeBps 为以基点 计的协议手续费（默认 300，即 3%；硬上限 1000，即 10%）。

胜负判定

closePrice > lockPrice   →  看涨方获胜
closePrice < lockPrice   →  看跌方获胜
closePrice == lockPrice  →  平局  →  该轮作废，全额退款，零手续费

哪些情况会导致轮次作废（全额退款、零手续费）

  • 平局——closePrice == lockPrice
  • 单边盘——up == 0 || down == 0：没有交易对手，因此无从赢起，也不会拿走任何人的钱
  • 锁定或结算时预言机数据不可用（过期、非正数、或轮次 ID 被重放）
  • 结算窗口 lockTs + bufferSeconds 已过而仍未结算
  • 管理员在该轮进行期间调用了 pause()

作废轮次会把每个参与者的本金原数退回。就赔付而言，「作废」与「已结算」互斥：不存在既可领奖又可退款的 轮次，也不存在两者皆不可、资金卡死的轮次。

其余情况——平价池分配

fee        = losePool * feeBps / 10000     ← 手续费只从输方资金池扣取
rewardPool = winPool + losePool - fee
rewardBase = winPool
payout     = userStake * rewardPool / rewardBase
           = userStake + userStake/winPool * losePool * (1 - feeBps/10000)

为什么手续费只从输方资金池扣取

链上常见的实现是按总池收取手续费，这实际上悄悄地对赢家自己的本金征了税。以 1,000 / 1,000 的 盘口、3% 手续费为例，赢方的 100 本金在「按总池收费」下只拿回 194，而在我们的 「只对输方池收费」下拿回 197。赢家拿到的钱永远不会少于本金，界面显示的赔率也正是 实际兑付的赔率。这是一处有意为之、对用户更有利的偏离。

界面展示的赔率

同一份信息，用两款对标产品各自的语汇表达。odds(epoch) 返回以基点表示的倍率， 10000 即 1.0000 倍；只要有一边尚无下注，就返回 (0, 0)。 

upMultiple    = (up + down * (1 - fee)) / up      币安式「赔付倍率」
upImpliedProb = 1 / upMultiple                    Polymarket 式「份额价格」，0..1

06Oracle rules预言机规则

Both the strike and the settlement come from a Chainlink AggregatorV3 feed read on-chain, at 8 decimals. A read is usable only if every one of these holds:

  • latestRoundData() does not revert (the call is wrapped in try/catch)
  • answer > 0
  • updatedAt != 0 and updatedAt <= block.timestamp — no future prints
  • block.timestamp - updatedAt <= oracleMaxAge — the print is fresh enough
  • roundId > lastOracleRoundId for this market — strictly monotonic, which blocks the replay of an aggregator round that was already consumed
  • the call itself arrives no later than lockTs + bufferSeconds

If a read is unusable at lock or at close, the affected round is voided and becomes fully refundable. The contract never falls back to a stale price, an interpolated price, or an admin-supplied price.

Testnet uses a relayed feed, and why

BSC testnet's own Chainlink feeds update far too infrequently to drive 5-minute rounds — BNB/USD was measured 1,480 seconds stale on 2026-08-26, which would void every short round and prove nothing. Testnet therefore deploys RelayAggregator, a Chainlink-shaped feed that the keeper pushes a real spot price into. It keeps testnet behaviour representative of mainnet. It is a testnet-only contract and the deploy script refuses to put it on mainnet.

  • 相对本市场满足 roundId > lastOracleRoundId——严格单调递增，杜绝重放已被消费过的 聚合器轮次
  • 调用本身发生在 lockTs + bufferSeconds 之前

若锁定或结算时读取不可用，相关轮次会被作废并转为全额可退款。合约绝不会退而使用过期价格、插值价格， 或由管理员手工提供的价格。

测试网为何使用中继喂价

BSC 测试网自带的 Chainlink 喂价更新频率过低，根本无法支撑 5 分钟轮次——2026-08-26 实测 BNB/USD 已经 陈旧了 1,480 秒，这会让每一轮短周期市场都作废，什么也验证不了。因此测试网改为部署 RelayAggregator：一个与 Chainlink 接口一致、由 keeper 推送真实现货价格的喂价合约， 使测试网行为与主网保持一致。它是仅限测试网的合约，部署脚本拒绝将其部署到主网。

The non-custodial invariant

The contract tracks outstanding, an upper bound on the user funds it still owes, and this must hold at every block. It is enforced by a Foundry invariant suite, not just asserted here: 

assetBalance(market) >= outstanding + treasuryAmount

outstanding = Σ unsettled stakes + Σ unclaimed payouts

Funds only ever leave by pull payment — the user calls claim(epochs[]). The contract never pushes money to a user during settlement, so one hostile or broken recipient can never block settlement for everyone else.

非托管不变量

合约用 outstanding 记录它仍欠用户的资金上界，下面这个关系必须在每个区块都成立。 它由 Foundry 不变量测试实际强制验证，而不只是写在文档里： 

assetBalance(market) >= outstanding + treasuryAmount

outstanding = Σ 未结算的下注 + Σ 未领取的赔付

资金只通过拉取式提款离开合约——由用户自己调用 claim(epochs[])。 合约在结算过程中从不主动向用户打款，因此单个恶意或异常的收款地址永远无法阻塞其他所有人的结算。

08Contract architecture合约架构

UpDownMarketBase (abstract) rounds · betting · settlement · claims · admin · oracle ├─ UpDownMarketERC20 settlement asset is an ERC20 (USDT on BSC, 18 decimals) └─ UpDownMarketNative settlement asset is native BNB UpDownRegistry on-chain directory of markets — one address the UI reads RelayAggregator (testnet) keeper-fed, Chainlink-shaped price feed TestUSDT (testnet) faucet USDT, 18 decimals, matching BSC-USDT
  • Solidity 0.8.28 with OpenZeppelin 5 — Ownable2Step, Pausable, ReentrancyGuard, SafeERC20.
  • The registry is deliberately a registry and not a factory: embedding market creation bytecode would push it past the 24 KB contract-size limit, and markets are deployed and source-verified individually anyway.
  • ERC20 deposits credit the measured balance delta, so a fee-on-transfer asset can never under-collateralise a round.
  • Ownership transfers are two-step (Ownable2Step), so a typo in an address can never orphan a market.
  • The admin is intended to be a multisig behind a Timelock at mainnet launch.

  • 这里刻意采用注册表而非工厂：把市场创建字节码内嵌进去会超过 24 KB 的合约体积上限， 而且每个市场本来就要单独部署与源码验证。

  • 主网上线时，管理员身份应为时间锁背后的多签钱包。

09Off-chain components链下组件

Keeper
TypeScript + viem. Calls executeRound() once per interval with retry, gas bumping, balance alerting and an idempotent catch-up path. On testnet it also relays a real spot price into RelayAggregator. Because of the permissionless fallback, a keeper outage degrades to refunds — never to loss.
Web
Next.js + wagmi + viem. Live round card with countdown, odds shown in both vocabularies, bet UP/DOWN, an open-position and claim list, and round history read from event logs.
Deploy scripts
Foundry. Deterministic deploy, BscScan source verification, and a JSON deployment artifact per chain id that the keeper and the web app both read.
Keeper（轮次执行机器人）
TypeScript + viem。每个 interval 调用一次 executeRound()，具备重试、 Gas 提价、余额告警与幂等追平逻辑。在测试网上还负责把真实现货价格中继进 RelayAggregator。由于存在无许可兜底，keeper 停机只会降级为退款，绝不会造成损失。
Web（前端）
Next.js + wagmi + viem。含倒计时的实时轮次卡片、以两种语汇展示的赔率、看涨/看跌下注、 持仓与可领取列表，以及基于事件日志的历史轮次记录。
部署脚本
Foundry。确定性部署、BscScan 源码验证，并按链 ID 输出一份 JSON 部署产物，供 keeper 与前端共同读取。

10Definition of done完成标准

  1. forge build clean; forge test green, including the fuzz and invariant suites.
  2. The non-custodial invariant holds under invariant testing, not just in review.
  3. Deployed and source-verified on BSC testnet (97), with a real end-to-end round — bet both sides, lock, close, claim — proven by on-chain transaction hashes.
  4. The web app is publicly reachable and driving that testnet deployment.
  5. Cross-vendor review returns APPROVED with an empty OPEN list.
  6. Mainnet (56) deployment is a separate, owner-gated step. It spends real funds and is irreversible, so it requires the owner's explicit go-ahead and a funded deployer key. Nothing in this repository deploys to mainnet on its own.
  1. forge build 无警告通过；forge test 全绿，含模糊测试与不变量测试套件。
  2. 非托管不变量在不变量测试下成立，而不仅仅是在评审中口头确认。
  3. 在 BSC 测试网（97）完成部署与源码验证，并跑通一轮真实的端到端流程——双边下注、 锁定、结算、领取——以链上交易哈希为证。
  4. 前端可公开访问，并驱动该测试网部署。
  5. 跨厂商交叉评审返回 APPROVED，且 OPEN 列表为空。
  6. 主网（56）部署是一个独立的、需业主授权的步骤。 它会花费真实资金且不可撤销，因此必须获得业主明确许可并配备已充值的部署私钥。本仓库中的任何流程 都不会自行部署到主网。
