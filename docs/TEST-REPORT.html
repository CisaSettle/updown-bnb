在最新部署的 ETH/USD 5 分钟市场上跑了一整轮真实交易：两个账户对赌，等 keeper 锁定、等它结算、然后领取，逐项核对算术。

这一轮把最要紧的那条性质演示得很干净：押 300 拿回 397，也就是本金 300 一分不少，加上净赢 97。手续费从输的那 100 里扣，不是从总池扣——所以赢家拿到的钱不可能低于自己的本金。报价赔率与实际到账逐 wei 相等，输家既不能领取也不能退款，链上调用直接回滚。

This round demonstrates the property that matters most, cleanly: a 300 stake returned 397 — the full 300 principal, plus 97 of winnings. The fee came out of the losing 100, not the total pool, which is why a winner can never be paid less than their own stake. The quoted odds equalled the realised payout to the wei, and the loser could neither claim nor refund; the call reverts on chain.

复核发现与处置

Findings, and what happened to them

这一节是这份报告真正的内容。工作方式是一方写、另一方审，两个不同厂商的模型互审，写的一方不能给自己放行。

会导致资金损失fund loss   由 Codex 在第 7 轮发现found by Codex, round 7

代领功能可以被抢跑，把合约账户的赔付永久锁死

The collect-on-behalf function could be front-run to strand a contract's winnings for good

为了回答"为什么不自动领取"，我加了 claimFor，让任何人都能自掏 gas 替别人领取。Codex 指出：一个只有下注和 claimTo、没有任意转账能力的合约账户赢了之后，正要把赔付转到一个安全地址，攻击者可以抢先调用 claimFor，把代币打进该合约本身并标记为已领取；它自己的 claimTo 随即回滚，钱永远卡在那里。攻击者一分钱也偷不到，却能让受害者归零。

To answer "why isn't collecting automatic", I added claimFor, letting anyone spend their own gas to collect for someone else. Codex pointed out that a contract account which can bet and call claimTo but cannot make arbitrary transfers — about to route its winnings somewhere safe — can be front-run: the attacker calls claimFor first, the tokens land at the contract itself, the position is marked claimed, and its own claimTo then reverts. The money is stuck there permanently. The attacker steals nothing and still zeroes the victim.

我的第一版补丁想只对普通钱包开放，用 code.length == 0 来区分账户类型。Codex 在下一轮证明这个判据修不好：一个地址在构造函数还没跑完时、以及自毁之后，都会报告"没有代码"，所以一个合约可以在自己的 constructor 里下注、随即自毁，事后看起来和普通钱包毫无区别；extcodehash 在同一窗口里同样是零。EVM 里根本没有可靠办法回答"这是什么账户"。

My first patch tried to allow it for plain wallets only, telling account kinds apart with code.length == 0. Codex showed in the next round that this test cannot be trusted: an address reports no code both while its constructor is running and after it has self-destructed, so a contract can take a position from inside its own constructor, self-destruct, and afterwards be indistinguishable from a wallet. extcodehash is zero in the same window. There is no opcode that reliably answers "what kind of account is this".

最终做法是不再猜：任何账户都必须自己发一笔交易开启 setAutoClaimOptIn(true)，可随时关闭。合约不去判断你是什么，它问你。

The resolution is to stop guessing: every account must opt in itself, in its own transaction, with setAutoClaimOptIn(true), revocable the same way. The contract does not decide what you are; it asks.

回归测试：Pinned by: test_aContractHolderCannotBeSweptIntoLosingItsPayoutRoute、test_aCodelessAddressIsStillNotSweptWithoutAskingToBe（用 vm.etch 复现自毁后的无代码状态）。两条都验证过：把防护改回旧写法它们会失败，装回去则通过。Both were verified to fail against the old guard and pass against the new one.

会显示错误结论wrong verdict shown   由 Codex 在第 8 轮发现found by Codex, round 8

前端的价格证明会把链上认可的轮次判成"无法验证"

The price-proof panel called rounds unverifiable that the chain settles happily

合约判断一笔报价是不是"边界前最后一笔"的方式，是去读它的下一个轮次编号；如果那次读取回滚，Solidity 的 try/catch 把这个"已完成的否定读取"当作终局证明。我写的链下镜像只在人为的编号上限处理了这种情况，漏掉了真实场景：一个 phase 停在比如本地编号 900，读 901 会回滚——链上判为 proven，页面却显示"未能验证"。Codex 修正了镜像，并区分了"读了但不存在"与"还没读到"这两件完全不同的事。

The contract decides whether a print is the last one before the boundary by reading the next round id; if that read reverts, Solidity's try/catch treats the completed negative read as proof of finality. My off-chain mirror only handled this at the artificial id ceiling and missed the ordinary case: a phase that simply ends at, say, local id 900, where reading 901 reverts. The chain proves it; the page said it could not be verified. Codex corrected the mirror and separated "read and absent" from "not read yet" — two different answers that had been collapsed into one.

我的复核：My review of the fix: 我逐条核对了接线（wagmi 把回滚报告为 failure，正是需要的区分），并验证了它新增的测试确有效力——把关键那一行改回我的写法，5 条测试失败。这条修复由我批准，方向与其它几条相反：这次是 Codex 写、我审。I traced the wiring — wagmi reports a reverted read as failure, which is exactly the distinction needed — and verified the new tests have teeth: reverting the decisive line to my version fails five of them. I approved this one; it runs the other way round, Codex writing and me reviewing.

会抵消合约的防护defeated a contract defence   整改工作流remediation workstream

市场一暂停，keeper 就不干活了——这在链下抵消了合约刚修好的东西

The keeper went silent on pause, undoing off-chain the very thing the contract had just fixed

合约特意让 executeRound 不受暂停影响，这样已经锁定的轮次照常按真实价格结算——一个眼看要输才去按暂停的管理员什么也捞不到。但 keeper 是唯一会去转这个曲柄的东西，而它一看到 paused 就直接返回。结果是：暂停、等结算窗口过期、全员退款——那条被堵上的路又通了。现在，一个已锁定轮次未结算的暂停市场，会被完全按未暂停对待，直到那一轮结算完毕；/healthz 也新增了 paused 状态并区分 pending 与 missed。

The contract deliberately leaves executeRound outside the pause, so a round that has already locked settles through at its true price — an owner who watches the settlement print land, sees they have lost and hits pause gains nothing. But the keeper is the only thing that turns that crank, and it returned early the moment paused was true. Pause, let the settlement window elapse, everybody is refunded: the door was open again. A paused market with an unsettled locked round is now driven exactly as an unpaused one until that round settles, and /healthz gained a paused state that distinguishes pending from missed.

你报告的reported by you

手机上下注按钮根本看不见

The bet buttons were invisible on a phone

两个容器写了 grid 但只带断点前缀的列定义（lg:grid-cols-2），基础断点没有任何 grid-cols-*。没有显式列模板时，隐式轨道按 max-content 计算：一张 341px 宽的卡片，它唯一那一列被算成 1748px；外层的 overflow-hidden 把超出部分裁掉，所以整页并不横向滚动（看起来正常），而按钮被画在屏幕之外、直接消失。

Two containers had grid with only a breakpoint-prefixed column definition (lg:grid-cols-2) and no base grid-cols-*. With no explicit template, the implicit track sizes to max-content: a 341px card resolved its single column to 1748px, and the parent's overflow-hidden clipped the excess — so the page did not scroll sideways and looked fine, while the buttons were drawn off-screen and simply vanished.

我最初的判断是错的（我怀疑无条件的 grid-cols-2 缺 min-w-0，但 Tailwind 3.4 的 grid-cols-2 本来就是 minmax(0,1fr)）。所以我停止猜测，改用浏览器调试协议在 375px 下逐个元素测量，直接量出那个 1748px 的元素并回溯它的祖先链。

My first diagnosis was wrong — I suspected unconditional grid-cols-2 without min-w-0, but Tailwind 3.4's grid-cols-2 is already minmax(0,1fr). So I stopped guessing and measured every element at 375px through the browser's debugging protocol, which named the 1748px element outright and let me walk up its ancestors.

修复后实测：Measured after the fix: 375 / 390 / 414 px 三种宽度下横向溢出元素均为 0，scrollWidth 等于视口宽度，下注按钮完整落在可视区内；六个市场全部渲染。— zero overflowing elements at all three widths, scrollWidth equal to the viewport, bet buttons fully inside it, and all six markets rendering.

点"连接钱包"报错，而错误信息把真正的原因丢掉了

Connect failed, and the message threw away the only useful fact

你看到的是"出了点问题，重试一次"。那是兜底文案。查下去发现：所有未被识别的错误，只有英文界面能看到原因，中文界面一律替换成兜底句——原意是不给中文读者塞一句没翻译的英文，但代价是把唯一能指导行动的信息销毁了。而最可能的真实原因（MetaMask 的 -32002：已有一个弹窗在等你，往往藏在浏览器窗口后面）根本没有被识别。

You saw "something went wrong, try again". That is the fallback. Underneath: any unrecognised error reached a 中文 reader as the bare fallback with the reason dropped — the intent was not to hand a Chinese reader an untranslated English sentence, but the cost was destroying the one fact they could act on. And the most likely actual cause, MetaMask's -32002 (a popup is already waiting, usually behind the browser window), was not recognised at all.

现在 -32002、"已连接"、"没有钱包应答"都有各自的中文说明；仍未识别的错误会带上错误码——数字在任何语言里都不是英文散文，所以既守住了不混排的规矩，也没有把线索丢掉；同时原始错误始终写进 console。

Now -32002, "already connected" and "no provider answered" each say what to do; anything still unrecognised carries its error code — a number is prose in no language, so the no-mixed-languages rule holds and the clue survives — and the raw error always reaches the console.

这次做出的产品决定

Product decisions made here

决定Decision 理由Why
六个市场全部用 USDT 结算，包括 BNB All six markets settle in USDT, BNB included 原生 BNB 市场是另一种要持有、也要另行推理的东西。统一结算资产让六个盘口用同一个单位比较，且只需要一次授权。原生市场合约保留在代码里并继续被测试——这是部署选择，不是能力削减。 A native-BNB market is a different thing to hold and to reason about. One settlement asset means six books compared in one unit and a single approval path. The native contract stays in the tree, built and tested: this is a deployment choice, not a reduction in what the protocol supports.
前端搬到自有域名，底层仍用 GitHub Pages The frontend moved to an owned hostname, still served by GitHub Pages 两个模型都同意该离开个人 github.io 子路径。Codex 建议改用 Cloudflare Pages；我不同意其核心论据——它的方案仍把仓库和构建留在 GitHub，换个 CDN 送字节并不把 GitHub 移出关键路径。拥有域名本身才是可移植性保证：将来要换任何托管，只是改一条 DNS，用户看到的地址不变。 Both models agreed the personal github.io subpath had to go. Codex preferred Cloudflare Pages; I disagreed with its central argument — that plan keeps the repo and the build on GitHub anyway, so serving the bytes elsewhere does not take GitHub off the critical path. Owning the hostname is itself the portability guarantee: moving hosts later becomes a DNS change with no URL churn.
领取仍然是"你来取"，但任何人都可以替你付 gas Collecting stays a pull, but anyone may pay the gas 结算是一笔要为全轮所有人收尾的交易。如果它还得给每个赢家转账，只要有一个地址拒收，整轮就结算不了——攻击者花一笔最小注就能故意卡住所有人。所以合约只记账。便利叠在这个保证之上，而不是拿它去换。 Settlement is one transaction that closes the round for every bettor at once. If it also had to pay each winner, a single address that rejects the transfer would fail the whole thing — one minimum bet to stop a round on purpose. So the contract only writes down what it owes. The convenience is layered on top of that guarantee rather than traded against it.
